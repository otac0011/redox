# Egg timing: when, how fast, how long, how often

This page is about time. An egg ovulated next month started growing well before this season began, and the oocyte inside it has been held in meiotic arrest since before its owner was born. Each factor for egg quality acts on a particular stage of that long path. Some act in days and fade in days. Some need months to build. Some, like PFAS or age, outlast anything you can do before a planned pregnancy. The aim here is to say which is which, with the numbers that have actually been measured, and to flag where a number is an extrapolation.

All days below count back from ovulation (or egg retrieval in IVF). Negative numbers mean days after conception.

## The timing biology of the egg

**The resting pool (decades).** Every egg a woman will ovulate is already present at birth, as an oocyte arrested in the first meiotic division inside a primordial follicle. The pool only shrinks. By 30 about 12% of the pre-birth maximum remains, and by 40 about 3% [tegg-wallace2010]. The proteins that hold paired chromosomes together (cohesin) were loaded in fetal life and wear out over the decades of arrest. That is the main reason aneuploidy climbs with age [tegg-nagaoka2012, tegg-gruhn2019].

**Activation and early growth (more than ~200 to ~90 days before ovulation).** Primordial follicles leave the pool continuously, without needing FSH. This is called initial recruitment [tegg-mcgee2000]. It takes "several months" for one to reach the preantral stage (0.15 mm) [tegg-gougeon2010]. Nobody has measured this duration precisely in women, so the 200-day start used in our window is an extrapolated lower bound. The oocyte grows a great deal here and builds the large mitochondrial pool the embryo will later live on [tegg-maypanloup2016].

**The ~85-day path (about 90 to 20 days before ovulation).** Gougeon's histology work showed that the ovulatory follicle comes from a preantral cohort that formed its theca layer about 85 days before ovulation [tegg-gougeon1986]. Growing from 0.15 mm to 2 mm takes about 70 days [tegg-gougeon2010]. This slow phase is what the "three months before IVF" advice refers to. CoQ10, DHEA and weight-loss programmes are timed to cover it (60 days, 12-17 weeks and 12-24 weeks respectively) [tegg-xu2018, tegg-yeung2014, tegg-barad2006, tegg-einarsson2017].

**Recruitment and selection (about 20 to 2 days).** The next ovulatory follicle is picked from a cohort of 2-5 mm follicles at the end of the previous luteal phase, 15-20 days before ovulation [tegg-gougeon1986]. Antral follicles grow in two or three waves per cycle, and one becomes dominant by mid-follicular phase [tegg-baerwald2012]. IVF stimulation works entirely inside this window, in about 11-12 days of FSH [tegg-mourad2025]. The follicular fluid that bathes the egg is made now, and it mirrors current blood levels of insulin, lipids, CRP and other metabolites [tegg-valckx2012, tegg-robker2009].

**Final maturation (about 2 days).** The LH surge, or the IVF trigger, restarts meiosis. The first spindle forms and the first polar body is expelled. Ovulation follows on average 34 h after the surge begins, with a range of 22-56 h [tegg-erden2022]. Chromosome errors happen here, but their cause (worn cohesion) was set years earlier [tegg-gruhn2019, tegg-franasiak2014].

**Conception to implantation (0 to about 12 days after).** Conception needs intercourse in the six days ending on ovulation day [tegg-wilcox1995]. The embryo implants 6-12 days after ovulation, mostly on days 8-10. Late implantation predicts loss: 13% when implantation happened by day 9, 82% after day 11 [tegg-wilcox1999].

**Neural tube and organogenesis (about 3 to 10 weeks after).** The neural tube closes between days 22 and 26 after fertilisation [tegg-copp2015], often before a pregnancy is confirmed. Organs form through gestational week 10. Between gestational weeks 8 and 12 the placenta goes from low oxygen to full maternal blood flow, with a natural burst of oxidative stress [tegg-jauniaux2000, tegg-burton2011]. Steegers-Theunissen's "periconception period" spans all of this. It is about 5-6 months from oocyte growth to week 10, and one-carbon (folate, B12) metabolism shapes it throughout [tegg-steegerstheunissen2013, tegg-fleming2018].

## What is the oocyte sensitive to, and when?

- **Mitochondrial supply.** The pool is built during the long growth phase (months before ovulation) [tegg-maypanloup2016]. In aged mice, CoQ10 restored oocyte mitochondrial function [tegg-benmeir2015] and NAD+ precursors restored fertility [tegg-bertoldo2020]. In women, CoQ10 for 60 days before IVF raised egg and embryo numbers [tegg-xu2018]. A meta-analysis found higher clinical pregnancy but not live birth (OR 1.67, NS) [tegg-florou2020]. The one aneuploidy trial was stopped early and underpowered [tegg-bentov2014]. Timing argument: mitochondria are made during growth, so supplements aimed at them should start about 2-3 months ahead. The trials that exist used 60 days; none compared durations.
- **Spindle and meiosis.** These form in the last 1-2 days, but whether they work depends mostly on age-worn cohesion [tegg-gruhn2019]. Oxidative stress blocks maturation in vitro (H2O2 on mouse oocytes), and melatonin blocked that effect [tegg-tamura2008]. No human trial isolates this window.
- **Follicular fluid oxidative stress.** This is set in the final cycle. Higher follicular 8-OHdG went with more degenerate oocytes. Melatonin 3 mg or vitamin E taken during the IVF cycle lowered follicular oxidative-damage markers [tegg-tamura2008]. This is the clearest example of something that can change in the last cycle.
- **Metabolic environment.** It acts in both places. Follicular fluid tracks blood chemistry now [tegg-valckx2012]. But in mice, oocyte spindle and mitochondrial defects from a high-fat diet persisted after 8 weeks of normal diet, even though weight and glucose had normalised [tegg-reynolds2015, tegg-xinyan2025].

### A month before ovulation, or two weeks?

No trial has compared starting 4 weeks before with starting 2 weeks before. The biology gives a qualified answer:

- **One month before ovulation**, the future ovulatory follicle is a small (under 2 mm) preantral-to-early-antral follicle still growing slowly [tegg-gougeon2010]. Anything that acts on oocyte growth, mitochondrial build-up or follicle survival can still reach it, though only for its last month of growth. In IVF cohorts, alcohol in the month before was linked to failure to conceive [tegg-klonoffcohen2003].
- **Two weeks before**, the follicle has already been recruited [tegg-gougeon1986]. What remains open is the follicular fluid, LH pulsatility and the final maturation signal [tegg-valckx2012, tegg-alvero1998, tegg-tamura2008].
- So for anything aimed at **egg growth** (mitochondrial nutrients, androgens, weight, metabolic health), one month is clearly better than two weeks, and three months is better still. For anything aimed at **the follicle's fluid or the embryo** (melatonin, avoiding fasts, caffeine, alcohol, folate already on board), the last two weeks still matter fully.

### What can change in the last cycle, and what needs 3-4 months?

| Changes within the last cycle | Needs about 3-4 months or more |
|---|---|
| Follicular fluid oxidative markers (melatonin, vitamin E) [tegg-tamura2008] | Oocyte growth-phase exposures (CoQ10, DHEA, weight) [tegg-xu2018, tegg-yeung2014] |
| LH pulsatility and ovulation after deep fasting [tegg-loucks2003, tegg-alvero1998] | Red-cell folate steady state (median 36 weeks) [tegg-crider2019] |
| Caffeine and alcohol exposure (cleared in hours to a day) [tegg-knutti1982] | Iron repletion (about 3 months) [tegg-vonsiebenthal2023] |
| Stimulation dose and trigger | Thyroid iodine stores ("several months") [tegg-moleti2011] |
| Drug washout for semaglutide (5-7 weeks) is the exception: it must start 2 months ahead [tegg-fda-wegovy2021] | PFAS body burden (half-lives 2.7-5.3 years) [tegg-li2018] |

## Answers to the four questions

### 1. Windows

The egg is shaped across three nested windows:

1. a months-long growth phase (roughly 200 to 20 days before ovulation), where mitochondria, oocyte size and follicle survival are set;
2. the final cycle (20 to 2 days), where follicular fluid and hormonal signalling are set and can be changed quickly;
3. a 1-2 day maturation window whose outcome depends mostly on age.

The embryo then has its own windows: implantation by about day 10 [tegg-wilcox1999], neural tube closure by day 26 [tegg-copp2015], and the placental oxygen transition at 8-12 weeks of gestation [tegg-jauniaux2000]. In practice, start growth-phase interventions about 3 months ahead. Things that act on follicular fluid or the embryo can still be started in the last cycle.

### 2. Kinetics

- **Fast (hours to days):** caffeine, melatonin and alcohol clearance; LH disruption by deep fasting; iron absorption [tegg-knutti1982, tegg-harpsoe2015, tegg-loucks2003, tegg-stoffel2017].
- **Build and plateau (weeks to months):** plasma folate (3 months) [tegg-hao2008], ferritin (about 3 months) [tegg-vonsiebenthal2023], AMH recovery after stopping the pill (2 months) [tegg-landersoe2020], PCOS cycle regularity on inositol (trials of about 14 weeks) [tegg-gerli2003, tegg-greff2023].
- **Slow and still growing:** red-cell folate (36 weeks) [tegg-crider2019], weight loss and its metabolic effects [tegg-wilding2022], and ovulation recovery in PCOS (6-7 months) [tegg-kiddy1992].
- **Slow harms:** age, which builds over decades [tegg-wallace2010], and PFAS body burden.

### 3. Persistence

Some effects outlast the exposure, for different reasons:

- **Long half-lives:** PFAS take 2.7-5.3 years to halve [tegg-li2018]. Menstruation adds an elimination route, but only a small one [tegg-wong2014]. Plasma donation every 6 weeks for a year lowered PFOS modestly [tegg-gasiorowski2022].
- **Structural loss of follicles:** AMH after endometrioma surgery does not recover [tegg-murdock2025]. Chemotherapy cuts AMH by about 20-90% at a year depending on the regimen (76% after breast-cancer chemotherapy), with only partial recovery [tegg-balachandren2026]. Smoking raises early-menopause risk, and that excess fades only over years after quitting [tegg-zhu2018].
- **Damage carried in already-growing oocytes:** in mice, oocyte defects outlast diet reversal [tegg-reynolds2015], and malformations after cyclophosphamide normalise only after about 12 weeks [tegg-meirow2001].
- **Short tails:** the semaglutide washout is 5-7 weeks [tegg-fda-wegovy2021]. Weight lost on GLP-1 drugs largely returns within a year of stopping [tegg-wilding2022]. Fertility returns within a few cycles after the pill, and after 5-8 cycles following injectables [tegg-yland2020].

### 4. Frequency

- **Folic acid:** daily. Single and split doses gave the same blood levels, but 4 mg once a week was much less effective than 0.4 mg daily [tegg-hao2008].
- **Iron:** alternate days absorbs better per dose [tegg-stoffel2017], gives the same ferritin at equal total dose, and causes fewer side effects [tegg-vonsiebenthal2023].
- **Melatonin:** has a half-life of about 45 min [tegg-harpsoe2015]. IVF trials used it nightly or twice daily through the stimulation cycle [tegg-fernando2017]. No trial compared schedules.
- **Aspirin:** in the preconception EAGeR trial, any benefit appeared only with adherence on at least 4-5 days a week [tegg-naimi2021].
- **Caffeine:** clears within a day, so it is the daily total, not the timing, that matters.
- **No schedule comparisons:** none were found for DHEA, CoQ10, inositol or iodine.

## Factors owned elsewhere: egg-side timing

These are summarised in the companion file `timing-egg-extra.json`.

- **Smoking.** Tobacco markers reach follicular fluid [tegg-benedict2011]. Smoking at the time of IVF roughly halves the odds of live birth (OR 0.54) [tegg-waylen2009]. Over years, smoking speeds follicle loss. Early-menopause risk falls with time since quitting and matches never-smokers after more than 10 years [tegg-zhu2018]; light smokers who quit by 25 matched never-smokers [tegg-whitcomb2018]. Stop at least 3 months ahead (extrapolated from the 85-day path). Stopping later still removes exposure from the final cycle and the embryo.
- **Alcohol.** It clears within hours, but the IVF data point to the month before [tegg-klonoffcohen2003]. Four or more drinks a week before IVF lowered live birth odds [tegg-rossi2011]. Fewer than 14 a week did not change natural fecundability [tegg-mikkelsen2016]. Drinking in pregnancy is linked to miscarriage in a dose-dependent way [tegg-sundermann2019]. Stopping before the conception cycle covers the weeks before a pregnancy is recognised.
- **SSRIs.** No lead time is needed for the egg. SSRI-only use was not linked to IVF live birth [tegg-cesta2016], and first-trimester use was not linked to cardiac defects after adjustment [tegg-huybrechts2014]. Stopping near conception was followed by relapse in 68% of women [tegg-cohen2006]. Any change therefore needs planning: fluoxetine and its metabolite take weeks to wash out [tegg-preskorn1997]. Domar and colleagues argue the opposite case, that SSRIs carry pregnancy risks without fertility benefit [tegg-domar2013].
- **Chemotherapy and radiation.** Conceiving within a year of starting chemotherapy was linked to more preterm birth; conceiving later was not [tegg-hartnett2018]. In mice, eggs that were growing during cyclophosphamide gave more failures and malformations for about 12 weeks [tegg-meirow2001]. AMH recovers partly between 6 and 24 months [tegg-balachandren2026, tegg-mailliez2022]. Freezing eggs or embryos before treatment is the standard option [tegg-oktay2018]. High-dose pelvic radiation raises stillbirth regardless of the wait [tegg-signorello2010].

## Where the evidence is contested

- **Does lead time matter for androgens?** Gleicher and Barad report gains that grow with 1-4 months of DHEA [tegg-gleicher2010, tegg-barad2006], and one small RCT reported higher live birth [tegg-wiser2010]. A 12-week placebo RCT was null [tegg-yeung2014], and Cochrane finds little or no live-birth effect [tegg-naik2024]. Polyzos argues that 5-21-day testosterone courses miss the early stages where androgens act [tegg-polyzos2018]. A small RCT found 3-4 weeks of testosterone better than 2 [tegg-kim2014].
- **Weight loss before IVF.** The small 12-week trial was strongly positive [tegg-sim2014], and a meta-analysis found more pregnancies [tegg-best2017]. Three large trials found no live-birth gain after 12 weeks to 6 months [tegg-einarsson2017, tegg-mutsaerts2016, tegg-legro2022]. The exception is anovulatory PCOS [tegg-legro2015, tegg-legro2016]. Mouse data suggest oocyte damage from obesity outlasts weight normalisation [tegg-reynolds2015].
- **Caffeine before conception.** One cohort found preconception caffeine harmless and early-pregnancy caffeine linked to miscarriage [tegg-hahn2015]. Another linked more than two drinks a day before conception, in either partner, to loss [tegg-bucklouis2016]. Leviton argues the whole miscarriage link is confounded by nausea [tegg-leviton2002].
- **Aspirin start time.** One meta-analysis favours starting by 16 weeks [tegg-roberge2017]; an individual-participant meta-analysis found start time irrelevant [tegg-meher2017].
- **Antioxidants for the placental window.** The oxidative-burst model [tegg-jauniaux2000] suggested earlier starts. A trial starting at 9-16 weeks was still null [tegg-roberts2010].
- **Folate lead time.** The optimal start is about 1.1 months before conception for non-cardiac defects but 2.2 months for heart defects [tegg-dong2023]. Red-cell kinetics argue for longer still [tegg-crider2019].
- **AMH on contraception.** Estimates of suppression range from about 24% (cross-sectional, from a testing company's data) [tegg-hariton2021] to about 35% (prospective washout) [tegg-landersoe2020].
- **PFAS and fertility.** Some associations may be reverse causation [tegg-whitworth2012], against a preconception cohort that still found lower fecundability [tegg-cohen2023].
- **Inositol.** Positive meta-analyses [tegg-greff2023] stand beside a retracted placebo trial [tegg-gerli2007], a retracted review [tegg-papaleo2025] and a 2025 expression of concern on the 2003 trial [tegg-gerli2003]. Cochrane rates the evidence very low [tegg-showell2018].
- **Storage time of frozen embryos.** One large analysis found no effect [tegg-cobo2024]; another found worse outcomes with longer storage [tegg-zhang2021].
- **GLP-1 washout.** The 2-month rule rests on pharmacokinetics and animal data [tegg-fda-wegovy2021]. Human cohorts are so far reassuring [tegg-cesta2024, tegg-ozbek2026, tegg-hviid2026].

## Practical bottom line

**Start first, about 3+ months ahead:**
- folic acid (0.4-0.8 mg daily; earlier is better for red-cell folate) [tegg-uspstf2023, tegg-crider2019];
- correct iron deficiency (alternate-day dosing) [tegg-vonsiebenthal2023];
- iodine-containing prenatal or iodised salt [tegg-moleti2011];
- stop smoking [tegg-zhu2018];
- for anovulatory PCOS, aim for about 5-7% weight loss over 16 weeks [tegg-legro2015];
- stop semaglutide at least 2 months before trying [tegg-fda-wegovy2021];
- if choosing DHEA or CoQ10 despite weak evidence, they need 2-3 months [tegg-yeung2014, tegg-xu2018].

**Can wait for the last cycle:**
- melatonin, if used at all, from the start of stimulation [tegg-fernando2017];
- avoiding multi-day fasts in the follicular phase [tegg-alvero1998];
- caffeine kept moderate (it clears in a day) [tegg-knutti1982];
- alcohol stopped by the conception cycle at the latest, ideally a month ahead [tegg-klonoffcohen2003].

**Needs to be daily:** folic acid. Iron works as well or better on alternate days. Aspirin, when prescribed in pregnancy, is daily.

**Can't be fixed in time:** age (freeze earlier if that is the plan [tegg-cobo2016]), PFAS body burden [tegg-li2018], follicles lost to surgery or chemotherapy.

## What we don't know

- How long primordial-to-preantral growth takes in women. Gougeon's estimate is qualitative.
- Whether a 1-month lead is better than a 2-week lead for any intervention. No trial compared lead times.
- Whether the oocyte effects of obesity reverse in women, and how fast. The persistence data come from mice.
- Dose-schedule comparisons for CoQ10, DHEA, inositol, melatonin and iodine.
- How fast follicular fluid recovers after quitting smoking. Follicular cotinine has been measured, but recovery after cessation has not.
