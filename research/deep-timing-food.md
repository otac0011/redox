# Timing of foods, polyphenols and Nrf2 activators: how fast, how long, how often

This page covers the time course of the diet factors on Redox Compass: sulforaphane and other isothiocyanates, polyphenol foods and supplements, tea, coffee, cocoa, and whole diet patterns. For each one it asks four things. How soon does an effect appear? When does it level off? How long does it last after you stop? Does it need to be daily? The owner's main question is whether Nrf2 activation from broccoli sprouts lasts long enough that it need not be daily. That comes first. Every citation was checked against its PubMed record in this session, and none of the cited papers is retracted. The 2018 PREDIMED paper is the corrected republication of a retracted 2013 report.

## The short answers

- **Sulforaphane is a pulse, but its effect has a memory of about 1-3 days.** The molecule peaks in blood about an hour after a dose and has a half-life of about 2 hours [tfood-ye2002, tfood-vermeulen2008]. The enzymes it switches on are long-lived proteins. Protection in human skin was still 32% two days after the last dose and 10% at three days [tfood-talalay2007]. In the Qidong crossover trial, detox markers were back to baseline after a 5-day break [tfood-kensler2012].
- **Daily dosing works and does not wear off.** In Qidong, benzene detoxification was up 61% on day 1 and stayed there for 12 weeks [tfood-egner2014]. Daily doses also build on each other: human skin NQO1 rose 1.5-fold after one dose and 4.5-fold after three daily doses [tfood-dinkovakostova2007].
- **No human trial has compared daily with every-other-day sulforaphane.** Every other day probably keeps a good part of the effect, but at a lower level than daily. A gap of three or more days loses most of it. This is an extrapolation from the decay data above.
- **Most polyphenol effects follow the last dose.** Cocoa, blueberries, citrus, tea, coffee and nitrate-rich greens act within hours. Daily use raises the baseline over days to weeks, and the gain is gone within about a week of stopping [tfood-heiss2007, tfood-mcdermott2020, tfood-kapil2015].
- **Diet patterns act on two timescales.** Blood pressure on DASH drops within a week [tfood-juraschek2017], and the gut microbiome shifts within a day [tfood-david2014]. Hard outcomes such as heart attacks and cognition take years, and trial evidence for them is mixed [tfood-estruch2018, tfood-barnes2023].
- **For conception, the lead time is set by biology, not by the food.** Nut and Mediterranean-diet trials improved sperm after 12-16 weeks, about one sperm cycle [tfood-robbins2012, tfood-salashuetos2018, tfood-montano2022]. A 6-week Mediterranean-style intervention before IVF already changed how embryos developed [tfood-kermack2020].

## 1. The biology of timing for food compounds

### Three kinds of clock

1. **The compound's own half-life (hours).** Most food bioactives are cleared within a day:
   - sulforaphane: half-life about 1.8-2.6 h [tfood-ye2002, tfood-vermeulen2008]
   - tea catechins: 1.7-3.4 h [tfood-lee2002]
   - olive-oil hydroxytyrosol: 2.4 h [tfood-mirocasas2003]
   - curcumin conjugates: about 7 h [tfood-vareed2008]
   - resveratrol metabolites: about 9 h [tfood-walle2004]

   Some compounds that gut bacteria must first transform last longer:
   - flax-derived enterolactone: peak at about 20 h, half-life about 13 h [tfood-kuijsten2005]
   - anthocyanin breakdown products: half-lives of 12-52 h, still in blood at 48 h [tfood-czank2013]
   - citrus flavanones: peak late (5-7 h) and gone by 24 h [tfood-manach2003]

   Across 97 studies, polyphenol metabolites reach only 0-4 µmol/L in plasma [tfood-manach2005].

2. **The response's own lifetime (hours to days).** Nrf2 itself turns over in under 20 minutes [tfood-kobayashi2004]. The proteins it induces last much longer: wild-type NQO1 has a half-life of over 18 hours in cells [tfood-siegel2001]. In human cells, a 24-hour sulforaphane exposure gave protection that kept rising for a day or two after the sulforaphane was washed out, then returned to control over the following days [tfood-gao2001]. This is the "memory" that outlasts the molecule.

3. **The turnover of the tissue or marker being measured (weeks to years).** HbA1c reflects about 3 months of glucose, so legume trials report at 3 months [tfood-jenkins2012]. Sperm take about 64 days (42-76) from labelled precursor to ejaculate [tfood-misell2006]. Plaque and cognition change over years [tfood-estruch2018, tfood-morris2015].

### Why "a pulse" can still matter

The induced enzymes act catalytically, so a short exposure can leave a protective state that lasts far longer than the molecule. Topical sulforaphane has a tissue half-life of only 1-2 hours, yet UV protection was still present 2-3 days later "because they depend on the synthesis of long-lived proteins" [tfood-talalay2007]. Most polyphenol effects on blood vessels, by contrast, track the circulating metabolites themselves:
- blueberry FMD rises at 1-2 h and 6 h, in step with plasma metabolites [tfood-rodriguezmateos2013]
- citrus improves microvascular function only at peak hesperetin [tfood-morand2011]

That is why these effects fade within a day.

## 2. The central question: does Nrf2 activation need to be daily?

### What the human data show

### Reading it

- **Onset is fast.** Gene induction is measurable within hours. A functional detox effect is present on day 1 [tfood-egner2014].
- **There is a dose threshold per day.** In Qidong, only the dose that produced about 25 µmol of urinary sulforaphane metabolites a day worked. Half that dose did not [tfood-chen2019]. Excretion rises linearly with dose and is very repeatable within a person [tfood-shapiro2001]. People also vary widely in how much glucoraphanin they convert, which drove the null result of an earlier Qidong trial [tfood-kensler2005].
- **Daily dosing gives a higher plateau than single doses.** The 1.5-fold versus 4.5-fold skin result [tfood-dinkovakostova2007] means each new dose lands before the previous one's enzyme has decayed.
- **The effect decays over 2-5 days.** That comes from human skin at 48-72 h [tfood-talalay2007], cells at about 4-5 days [tfood-gao2001], and the 5-day washout in Qidong [tfood-kensler2012]. Clinical effects that depend on this, such as H. pylori suppression and autism behaviour, also reverse after stopping [tfood-yanaka2009, tfood-singh2014].
- **There is no tolerance in practice.** A feedback loop exists in which Nrf2 switches on its own inhibitor, KEAP1 [tfood-lee2007]. It did not cause any loss of effect over 12 weeks of daily dosing in 291 people [tfood-egner2014]. We found no published evidence that later stages of the Qidong trials attenuated. Effects were stable from day 1 to week 12.
- **Splitting a dose does not help the markers.** 200 µmol once a day versus 100 µmol twice a day kept plasma levels higher later in the day but did not change HO-1, HDAC activity or p21 in blood cells [tfood-atwell2015].

### So: daily, every other day, or weekly?

- **Daily (or most days) is what is proven.** Every positive human trial dosed daily.
- **Every other day is plausible but untested in people.** Two pieces of evidence point that way: enzyme-mediated protection still largely present at 48 h [tfood-talalay2007], and an every-other-day regimen that worked through Nrf2 in diabetic mice for 3 months [tfood-xu2016]. Expect a lower average level of induction than with daily intake, because daily doses stack [tfood-dinkovakostova2007].
- **Weekly whole-food intake can still do something.** It works through slower, cumulative endpoints, not a sustained Nrf2 state. 400 g of high-glucoraphanin broccoli a week lowered LDL by 5-7% over 12 weeks [tfood-armah2015]. A weekly broccoli soup attenuated cancer-linked gene changes in the prostate over a year [tfood-traka2019].
- **More is not always better.** Permanent maximal Nrf2 activation kills mice lacking KEAP1 [tfood-wakabayashi2003]. That argues against stacking several concentrated products to keep Nrf2 "on" around the clock.

## 4. Answers to the four questions

### 4.1 Windows: does it matter whether you start a month or two weeks before?

For sperm, the relevant stretch is about 2-2.5 months, the time from new germ cells to ejaculated sperm [tfood-misell2006]. The older ~74-day figure may be off by about a week [tfood-amann2008].
- Nut trials ran 12 weeks (75 g/day walnuts [tfood-robbins2012]) and 14 weeks (60 g/day mixed nuts, which also lowered DNA fragmentation [tfood-salashuetos2018]).
- A 4-month Mediterranean diet plus exercise program improved sperm concentration, motility and morphology [tfood-montano2022].
- An 80 mg/day curcumin trial ran 10 weeks [tfood-alizadeh2018].

None measured at intermediate points, so we cannot say how much a 2-4-week start achieves. Starting about 3 months ahead covers every stage. A late start may still help the last stages if the food acts quickly (sulforaphane-type induction is on within a day), but that is untested for sperm.

For eggs, follicles grow for several months, including about 70 days from the preantral stage to 2 mm [tfood-gougeon2010]. Even so, 6 weeks of an omega-3, vitamin D and olive-oil intervention before IVF changed later embryo cleavage and day-3 embryo scores [tfood-kermack2020]. Lower Mediterranean-diet adherence before IVF was linked to lower live birth in women under 35 [tfood-karayiannis2018]. Habitual soy isoflavone intake was linked to higher ART live birth [tfood-vanegas2015], but that concerns long-standing diet, not a timed start. Resveratrol is the one food-derived supplement with a timing harm: continuous use during embryo-transfer cycles was linked to lower pregnancy (OR 0.54) and more miscarriage (OR 2.60) [tfood-ochiai2019]. Stopping before that cycle matters more than starting early. In general, earlier is better (about 3 months for sperm, 3-4 months for eggs), but 6 weeks is not too late to bother.

### 4.2 Kinetics: fast, plateauing, or slowly growing?

- **Fast (hours to days):**
  - sulforaphane and watercress detox [tfood-egner2014, tfood-hecht1995]
  - cocoa, blueberry and citrus vascular effects [tfood-heiss2007, tfood-rodriguezmateos2013, tfood-morand2011]
  - coffee and DNA strand breaks [tfood-bakuradze2016]
  - nitrate and blood pressure [tfood-webb2008]
  - DASH and blood pressure [tfood-juraschek2017, tfood-conlin2000]
  - organic food and pesticide metabolites [tfood-gonzalez2023, tfood-hyland2019]
  - spices at a meal [tfood-li2010]
  - microbiome composition [tfood-david2014, tfood-wu2011]
- **Build and plateau (weeks):**
  - cocoa's fasting baseline rose over days 3-8 [tfood-heiss2007]
  - oxidized LDL with phenolic olive oil (3 weeks) [tfood-covas2006]
  - IL-6 on crucifers (2 weeks) [tfood-navarro2014]
  - orange juice and blood pressure (4 weeks) [tfood-morand2011]
  - coffee and DNA damage (4 weeks) [tfood-bakuradze2015]
  - watercress and DNA damage (8 weeks) [tfood-gill2007]
  - micellar curcumin blood levels (3 weeks) [tfood-kocher2016]
  - Note that DASH, unlike salt reduction, does not keep building after week 1 [tfood-juraschek2017].
- **Slow-build (months to years):**
  - flaxseed and blood pressure (6 months) [tfood-rodriguezleyva2013]
  - green tea and 8-OHdG in smokers (4 months) [tfood-hakim2003]
  - black tea and blood pressure (months, not a single day) [tfood-hodgson2013]
  - legumes and HbA1c (3 months) [tfood-jenkins2012]
  - PREDIMED cardiovascular events (about 5 years) [tfood-estruch2018]
  - MIND diet and cognition (cohort over 4.7 years) [tfood-morris2015]

### 4.3 Persistence: what outlasts the intake?

- **Short memory (1-5 days):** Nrf2-induced protection [tfood-talalay2007, tfood-gao2001, tfood-kensler2012], cocoa FMD (gone after 1 week) [tfood-heiss2007], and microbiome composition (back 2 days after a diet ends) [tfood-david2014].
- **Weeks:**
  - Watercress detox returned to near baseline 1-2 weeks after stopping [tfood-hecht1995].
  - Nitrate blood-pressure lowering drifted back during a 2-week washout [tfood-kapil2015].
  - Autism and H. pylori benefits from sulforaphane reversed over 4-8 weeks [tfood-singh2014, tfood-yanaka2009].
  - Green tea extract liver injury needed about a month to resolve after stopping (mean 32 days, range 13-92) [tfood-yu2017].
- **Lasting:**
  - Equol-producer status is a stable trait (82% concordance over 1-3 years) [tfood-frankenfeld2005].
  - Urolithin metabotypes are also stable [tfood-tomasbarberan2014].
  - In mice, fibre deprivation over several generations removed gut microbes that fibre alone could not bring back [tfood-sonnenburg2016]. This is a mechanistic warning that some microbiome losses may not be reversible.

### 4.4 Frequency: does it need to be daily?

- **Daily or most days:** anything whose effect tracks the last dose. That includes sulforaphane sources (1-3-day memory), cocoa, berries, citrus, tea, coffee, nitrate greens, fibre (microbiome follows the last 1-2 days), and the diet patterns.
  - The cocoa trial in peripheral artery disease illustrates the point. Walking improved by 42.6 m when tested 2.5 h after a dose, but only 18 m (not significant) at 24 h [tfood-mcdermott2020].
  - In COSMOS, a third of people assigned to cocoa did not reach expected flavanol biomarker levels, and biomarker-based analyses gave larger effects [tfood-ottaviani2025].
  - With no tolerance seen for sulforaphane over 12 weeks [tfood-egner2014], cocoa over 30 days [tfood-balzer2008] or nitrate over 4 weeks [tfood-kapil2015], there is no reason to cycle these.
- **A few times a week:** whole broccoli for LDL (400 g/week) [tfood-armah2015] and weekly glucoraphanin-rich soup [tfood-traka2019].
- **Per meal:** mustard powder on cooked crucifers [tfood-okunade2018], spices in cooking [tfood-li2010], moringa with a carbohydrate meal [tfood-leone2018].
- **Before an exposure:** Polypodium before sun [tfood-middelkamphup2004].
- **Split, not bolus, if you take it at all:** once-daily 800 mg EGCG made exposure climb more than 60% over 4 weeks, while 400 mg twice daily did not [tfood-chow2003]. Flax lignans reach steady state with intake 2-3 times a day [tfood-kuijsten2005].
- **Tolerance worth knowing:** tolerance to caffeine's blood-pressure effect builds and fades with a half-life of about an hour and is lost after brief abstinence [tfood-shi1993].

## Where the evidence is contested

- **How long Nrf2 activation lasts.** Cell and skin studies show enzyme-mediated protection persisting for days after sulforaphane is gone [tfood-gao2001, tfood-talalay2007, tfood-dinkovakostova2007]. The Qidong crossover found no carryover after a 5-day washout [tfood-kensler2012]. Both can be true: protection decays over roughly 2-4 days, so it is gone by day 5.
- **Tolerance.** The Nrf2-KEAP1 feedback loop predicts damping under constant stimulation [tfood-lee2007]. The 12-week Qidong trial found effects as large at week 12 as on day 1 [tfood-egner2014]. We found no published report of attenuation later in the Qidong trials.
- **Bolus or steady exposure.** Egner and colleagues suggest blending glucoraphanin (slow, steady) with sulforaphane (fast peak) [tfood-egner2011]. Split dosing raised plasma exposure but did not change blood-cell targets [tfood-atwell2015]. No outcome trial has settled it.
- **Long-term cocoa.** Short trials consistently improve FMD, with an inverted-U dose response and high risk of bias [tfood-sun2019]. COSMOS missed its primary endpoint after 3.6 years (HR 0.90) [tfood-sesso2022]. A biomarker re-analysis by trial investigators, two of them employed by Mars (which sells cocoa-flavanol products), argues that non-adherence and high background intake hid a larger effect [tfood-ottaviani2025].
- **PREDIMED.** The Mediterranean diet's event reduction accrued over about 5 years [tfood-estruch2018]. The trial was retracted and republished after randomization irregularities, and critics question how far the revised estimates can be trusted [tfood-agarwal2019].
- **MIND diet.** A cohort found slower decline over 4.7 years [tfood-morris2015]. The 3-year randomized trial found nothing against a calorie-matched control [tfood-barnes2023]. It is unresolved whether 3 years was too short or the cohort link was confounded.
- **Produce and oxidative damage.** Doubling fruit and vegetables for 14 days lowered oxidative DNA and lipid markers [tfood-thompson1999]. 600 g/day for 24 days did not [tfood-moller2003].
- **Microbiome reversibility.** Human feeding studies show fast, reversible change [tfood-david2014, tfood-wu2011]. Generational mouse data show cumulative, irreversible loss [tfood-sonnenburg2016]. An 8-week whole-grain trial produced no major microbiome shift at all [tfood-roager2019].

## Practical bottom line

1. **Start the slow ones first if you are planning conception.** A Mediterranean-style pattern with daily nuts (30-75 g) and olive oil should start about 3 months before trying for sperm, and 3-4 months for eggs [tfood-robbins2012, tfood-salashuetos2018, tfood-montano2022]. Six weeks still showed an effect on embryos [tfood-kermack2020]. Stop resveratrol before an embryo-transfer cycle [tfood-ochiai2019].
2. **Sulforaphane: aim for most days.** Daily is what worked, without tolerance [tfood-egner2014]. Missing a day loses little, because protection lasts about 2 days. Gaps of 3 or more days lose most of it [tfood-talalay2007, tfood-kensler2012]. Make each dose count by staying above the effective threshold (about 25 µmol a day of metabolites in Qidong) [tfood-chen2019]. There is no need to split it [tfood-atwell2015].
3. **Polyphenol foods: little and often beats occasional big doses.** Their vascular effects follow the last serving [tfood-heiss2007, tfood-rodriguezmateos2013, tfood-mcdermott2020]. A daily cup of berries [tfood-curtis2019], daily cocoa or tea, coffee spread through the day [tfood-bakuradze2016], and daily leafy greens [tfood-kapil2015] all fit.
4. **Diet patterns: blood pressure moves in a week, real outcomes take years.** Expect DASH to work within days [tfood-juraschek2017]. Don't expect cognition or heart benefits to show in months [tfood-barnes2023, tfood-estruch2018].
5. **Fibre: every day.** Your microbiome reflects the last couple of days of food [tfood-david2014].
6. **Avoid high-dose green tea extract.** Liver problems appear after about 3.5 months (median) and take about a month to resolve [tfood-yu2017, tfood-efsa2018]. Turmeric and curcumin products carry a 1-4-month latency for rare liver injury [tfood-halegoua2023].

## What we don't know

- No human trial has compared daily with every-other-day (or twice-weekly) sulforaphane, or any other Nrf2 activator, on any outcome.
- How long Nrf2 target enzymes stay raised in human blood cells, airway or other internal tissues after the last dose has not been measured. The best human figures come from one-subject skin data [tfood-talalay2007].
- The half-life of induced HO-1, GCLC and the GST enzymes in human tissues in vivo is unknown. The >18 h NQO1 figure comes from cell lines [tfood-siegel2001].
- No fertility trial measured sperm or egg outcomes at intermediate times (for example 4 or 8 weeks), so the value of a late start is unknown.
- For most polyphenol foods, persistence after stopping has not been measured beyond the plasma kinetics.
- How fast blood pressure rises after stopping DASH, and whether the cardiovascular benefit of a Mediterranean diet persists after adherence stops, were not measured in the trials cited here.
