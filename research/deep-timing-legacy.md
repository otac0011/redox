# What lasts: the timing of legacy exposures, pollutants, medications and skin

Most of the factors in this group are things you are trying to *avoid* rather than things you are trying to take, and the single most useful question about any of them is: **if I stop today, when does it stop mattering?** The answers in this group span seven orders of magnitude. Bisphenol A is half gone from your blood in about six hours [tleg-thayer2015]. Cadmium is half gone in fourteen to twenty-four years [tleg-suwazono2009]. Both are "chemical exposures you can reduce", and advice that treats them the same is useless.

This page sorts the group by what actually persists. Three patterns recur:

1. **Fast chemicals that need continuous avoidance.** BPA, phthalates, dietary pesticides, acrylamide, heterocyclic amines. Urinary biomarkers fall by half to nine-tenths within one to three days of changing your food or packaging [tleg-rudel2011, tleg-oates2014], and rise again with the next canned meal [tleg-carwile2011]. There is no reservoir to drain and no credit to bank.
2. **Slow chemicals where today's decision barely touches today's body.** PFAS (half-lives 2.7-5.3 years) [tleg-li2018pfas], cadmium (14-24 years) [tleg-suwazono2009], bone lead (16 years) [tleg-nilsson1991], PCBs (11.5-15.5 years) [tleg-ritter2011]. For these, the only levers that work on a human planning horizon are preventing further intake and, for PFAS specifically, deliberately accelerating elimination [tleg-moller2024, tleg-gasiorowski2022].
3. **Structural damage that outlives everything.** UV photoageing and skin cancer. A single exposure raises collagen breakdown 58% within hours [tleg-fisher1997]; the cumulative result does not reverse, which is why the sunscreen trial's benefit was still visible a decade after the trial stopped [tleg-green2011].

## Question 1: Windows - does it matter whether you act a month or two weeks before conception?

For this group, yes, and the reason is usually chemistry rather than cell biology.

**Sperm.** A sperm takes about 74-90 days to make, and air-pollution studies that model exposure week by week across that window show the stages are not equivalent. Critical windows were weeks 6-12 of development for PM10, weeks 10-11 for ozone and weeks 0-5 for SO2 [tleg-sun2020]; a second cohort with repeated semen samples found ozone during meiosis and spermiogenesis hit morphology, while PM2.5 and ozone during *spermiation* - the last days before release - hit count, concentration and motility [tleg-russo2025]. That is the single most practically useful finding in this group for a man with two weeks' notice: **late reduction is not worthless**, because the final days have their own sensitive endpoint. Within-person data back the whole-window version: the same young men had more sperm DNA fragmentation after high-pollution periods [tleg-rubes2005].

For chemicals with fast kinetics the limiting factor is sperm development, not the chemical. Urinary phthalates and BPA fall in days [tleg-rudel2011], so a man who cleans up his food packaging 90 days out gets the full available benefit, and one who does it 14 days out covers only the last stage. For chemicals with slow kinetics the limiting factor is the chemical, and the lead time is years, not months: nothing you do three months before conception moves serum PFAS by more than a few percent [tleg-li2018pfas], and blood mercury needs two to three half-lives, i.e. four to six months, to fall substantially [tleg-yaginuma2012].

**Egg.** The one clean window finding is from IVF: across 522 cycles, the association between air pollution and failure was concentrated in the **ovarian stimulation** period, where each interquartile rise in NO2 carried 42% higher odds of failing before retrieval, rather than in later stages [tleg-gaskins2019]. Exposure during the preceding months of follicle growth is what the phthalate cohort data describe - top-quartile urinary DEHP metabolites were associated with fewer oocytes and about 19 percentage points lower live birth per cycle [tleg-hauser2016] - but those studies measure a spot urine, which for DEHP metabolites has poor month-to-month reproducibility [tleg-dewalque2015], so the window attribution is weak.

Two egg-side exposures have a genuinely short window. NSAIDs act locally on COX-2-dependent follicle rupture, so the sensitive period is the few days around the LH surge: periovulatory rofecoxib delayed rupture beyond 48 hours after the LH peak in four of six women [tleg-pall2001], continuous NSAIDs produced luteinized unruptured follicles in 35.6% of cycles versus 3.4% untreated [tleg-micu2011], and ovulation returned in the next cycle after stopping [tleg-smith1996]. Secondhand smoke, judged by IVF outcomes, also acts near implantation: passive smokers had implantation rates of 12.6% and pregnancy rates of 20.0%, versus 25.0% and 48.3% in non-smokers, *despite similar embryo quality* [tleg-neal2005].

And one exposure runs the wrong way through pregnancy: lead stored in the maternal skeleton is released during pregnancy and more so during lactation, supplying about a third of maternal blood lead, with bigger releases on low calcium intake [tleg-gulson2003]. Avoiding lead before conception is not enough if the skeleton already holds it.

## Question 2: Kinetics - what is fast, what plateaus, what keeps growing

**Hours.** Secondhand smoke needed 30 minutes to cut coronary flow velocity reserve from 4.4 to 3.4 [tleg-otsuka2001]. A meal cooked in used fryer fat cut flow-mediated dilation from 5.9% to 0.8% at four hours [tleg-williams1999]. A high-AGE meal cut it 36% at two to six hours [tleg-negrean2007]. Fine particles raised heart attack risk within the previous two hours [tleg-peters2001]. UV switched on collagenase within hours [tleg-fisher1997].

**Days.** Organic food drove urinary organophosphate metabolites to undetectable immediately and they stayed there until conventional food came back [tleg-lu2006]; a week cut total dialkylphosphates 89% [tleg-oates2014]. Three days of fresh unpackaged food cut urinary BPA 66% and DEHP metabolites 53-56% [tleg-rudel2011]. HEPA purifiers lowered 8-isoprostane, exhaled nitric oxide and blood pressure within 48 hours to a week [tleg-chen2015, tleg-wang2021]. Antibiotics flattened gut diversity in three to four days [tleg-dethlefsen2011]. Acetaminophen at 4 g/day raised ALT above three times normal within two weeks in 31-44% of healthy adults [tleg-watkins2006].

**Weeks that then plateau.** Trans fat's full lipid effect appeared within a three-week feeding period [tleg-mensink1990]. Statins' LDL, oxidized-LDL and F2-isoprostane reductions were present at one month [tleg-decaterina2002] and blood CoQ10 had already halved by day 30, with a significant fall at day 14 [tleg-rundek2004]. Plasma and red-cell linoleic acid shifted in the first two weeks and then stopped changing [tleg-hodson2014]. PPI-driven taxon shifts were established by four weeks [tleg-freedberg2015]. Red meat tripled TMAO over four weeks [tleg-wang2019tmao]. SGLT2 inhibitors are the surprise: a significant clinical benefit by 28 days [tleg-berg2021].

**Months.** Periodontal therapy: worse at 24 hours, better by 60 days, clearly better at 180 days for endothelial function [tleg-tonetti2007], with HbA1c down 0.43 points at 3-4 months [tleg-simpson2022]. Topical retinoids: 16 weeks to visible improvement [tleg-weiss1988], still improving at 48 weeks [tleg-olsen1997a]. Topical vitamin C: skin saturates in three days but visible change is judged at 3-6 months [tleg-pinnell2001, tleg-humbert2003]. Acrylamide haemoglobin adducts need about four months to re-equilibrate because they ride on the red cell [tleg-wilson2009].

**Years, still growing.** Statin event prevention: 2.5 years to avoid one major event per 100 people treated [tleg-yourman2021]. Sunscreen: photoageing measured over 4.5 years [tleg-hughes2013], squamous-cell tumours down 39% during the trial [tleg-green1999] and still down about 40% eight years later [tleg-vanderpols2006], melanomas halved over the following decade [tleg-green2011]. Adipose fatty-acid composition: triglyceride half-life 200-270 days [tleg-strawford2004], mean lipid age about 1.6 years [tleg-arner2011], and adipose linoleic acid correlating with diet only after five to six years [tleg-dayton1967]. Population-level air-pollution reduction: 0.61 years of life expectancy per 10 ug/m3 over two decades [tleg-pope2009].

## Question 3: Persistence - what outlasts the exposure, and why

| Why it persists | Example | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Long serum half-life (enterohepatic recirculation, protein binding) | PFOA, PFOS, PFHxS | Half gone in 2.7-5.3 y [tleg-li2018pfas] |
| Same, for lipophilic organics in fat | PCB-153, PCB-170, dioxins | 11.5-15.5 y [tleg-ritter2011]; years to decades [tleg-milbrath2009] |
| Sequestration in a slow tissue compartment | Lead in bone | 16 y bone half-time, with a 13-y blood component [tleg-nilsson1991] |
| Renal accumulation | Cadmium | Urinary half-life 13.6-23.5 y [tleg-suwazono2009] |
| Slow tissue turnover | Adipose fatty acids (including trans fats) | Triglyceride half-life 200-270 d [tleg-strawford2004] |
| Protein turnover sets the measurement window | Acrylamide haemoglobin adducts | ~4 months (red-cell lifespan) [tleg-wilson2009] |
| Ecological loss that does not recolonise | Gut species after antibiotics | 9 species absent at 180 d [tleg-palleja2018]; Bacteroides clones altered up to 2 y [tleg-jernberg2007] |
| Irreversible structural and genetic damage | UV photoageing, skin cancer | Decades; prevention benefit persists 8-10 y after the habit stops [tleg-vanderpols2006, tleg-green2011] |
| Unknown mechanism, unknown duration | Micro- and nanoplastics | Never measured [tleg-lane2026] |

Two persistence findings cut the other way and are worth knowing.

**Benefits can persist too.** The sunscreen trial is the strongest example of a prevented-damage benefit outliving the intervention [tleg-green2011, tleg-vanderpols2006]. SGLT2 inhibitors are the pharmacological example: after empagliflozin was stopped, cardiorenal benefit continued for up to about 12 months before the groups converged [tleg-empakidney2025].

**Stopping can hurt faster than starting helped.** Abruptly stopping a statin raised CRP and IL-6 above treated levels by days 3 and 7, independently of LDL [tleg-li2006statin]. Eight weeks of a PPI produced clinically relevant acid symptoms in 21-22% of previously healthy volunteers in the four weeks *after* withdrawal, versus 2-7% on placebo [tleg-reimer2009] - a rebound that is routinely mistaken for the return of the original disease.

**And some exposures cannot be reduced to zero.** On an acrylamide-minimised diet, volunteers still excreted a baseline that implies the body forms 0.3-0.4 ug/kg/day of acrylamide endogenously [tleg-goempel2017]. That sets a floor on what cooking changes can achieve.

## Question 4: Frequency - what has to be daily

**Must be continuous, because the half-life is hours to days:** BPA and phthalate avoidance [tleg-thayer2015, tleg-carwile2011], low-residue produce [tleg-lu2006], HEPA filtration [tleg-chen2015], topical vitamin C (three daily applications to saturate skin, 4-day tissue half-life afterwards) [tleg-pinnell2001], water filtration (every glass, with filter maintenance, since exhausted carbon can release PFAS back) [tleg-mackeown2024].

**Must be habitual rather than perfect, because tissue integrates over months:** acrylamide and heterocyclic amines from cooking [tleg-goempel2017, tleg-gongora2019], dietary fat type [tleg-strawford2004], red and processed meat [tleg-wang2019tmao].

**One factor has a real dosing-schedule trial.** After 48 weeks of daily tretinoin, three applications a week maintained and sometimes further improved photodamage, once a week did so less well, and stopping led to partial regression [tleg-olsen1997b]. Retinol 0.4% worked at up to three times weekly from the start [tleg-kafi2007]. This is the only factor in the group where "is every other day as good?" has been answered with a randomized comparison, and the answer is: most-days maintenance is enough once you have built the effect, but not to build it.

**One factor's schedule question is about risk, not benefit.** Do not start a ketogenic or very-low-carbohydrate diet while taking an SGLT2 inhibitor without supervision: euglycaemic ketoacidosis has followed a single dose, and within a week of starting such a diet [tleg-mistry2021].

**Tolerance and adaptation.** The clearest case is acetaminophen: 77% of people taking 4 g/day had no or only transient ALT elevation by day 16, and 47 of the 48 who did normalised by day 40 *while still taking the drug* [tleg-heard2014]. Statin muscle symptoms behave similarly in reverse: the excess is confined to year 1 (about 11 events per 1,000 person-years) and absent afterwards, and over 90% of muscle complaints on statins were not caused by the statin [tleg-ctt2022].

## Where the evidence is contested

**Does lowering a body burden do anything?** For PFAS we can now move the number - cholestyramine cut serum PFOS 60% in 12 weeks [tleg-moller2024], colesevelam 38% [tleg-andersson2025], plasma donation measurably over 12 months [tleg-gasiorowski2022] - but not one of these studies measured a health outcome. The same gap exists for every dietary avoidance intervention in this group [tleg-rudel2011, tleg-oates2014, tleg-hyland2019].

**How fast does BPA really clear?** Controlled dosing says about six hours and over 90% excreted in 24 hours [tleg-thayer2015, tleg-teeguarden2011]. NHANES says urinary BPA did not fall with longer fasting, implying substantial non-food exposure or storage, with a population half-life of about 43 hours [tleg-stahlhut2009]. The practical stakes are high: on the fast reading, three days of clean eating before a test is enough; on the slow reading, it is not.

**Do avoidance interventions reliably work?** One randomized "low-exposure" catered diet raised urinary DEHP metabolites about 25-fold because the ingredients themselves were contaminated [tleg-sathyanarayana2013], against clear reductions in two other studies [tleg-rudel2011, tleg-harley2016].

**How fast does cleaner air pay off at population level?** Dublin's coal ban was followed by 15.5% fewer respiratory and 10.3% fewer cardiovascular deaths [tleg-clancy2002]; the extended Irish analysis found respiratory but not cardiovascular or total mortality reductions [tleg-dockery2013]; a reanalysis attributed the original result to influenza epidemics and secular trends [tleg-wittmaack2007]. Smoking bans show the same pattern: 17% fewer heart attacks in a meta-analysis of local studies with the effect growing 26% per additional year [tleg-meyers2009], consistent Cochrane findings for acute coronary syndrome [tleg-frazer2016], but no significant short-term effect in US national data, where subsampling produced large *increases* as often as the published large decreases [tleg-shetty2010].

**Does the air-purifier marker effect mean anything?** Systolic blood pressure fell about 4 mmHg in one meta-analysis [tleg-walzer2020], 2.3 mmHg with no CRP or endothelial change and "very low" certainty in another whose authors explicitly warned against strong claims [tleg-xia2021], and CRP was 7% lower and IL-6 13% lower in a third [tleg-wittkopp2022]. The only hard-outcome trial, HEPA filters through pregnancy in a heavily polluted city, missed its primary endpoint [tleg-barn2018], and a year of indoor purification did nothing for semen quality in infertile men [tleg-paradisi2009].

**Does the ovary care about air pollution over the long run?** One real-world dataset linked NO2 to lower AMH [tleg-lamarca2020]; a 20-year cohort with up to five repeated AMH measurements found no clinically relevant association with any pollutant, or with age at menopause [tleg-zou2025].

**Are microplastics measurable at all?** A prominent study reported rising micro- and nanoplastic concentrations in decedent brains [tleg-nihart2025]; a published letter argues the concentrations are implausible because the polyethylene pyrolysis markers cannot be distinguished from body lipids [tleg-monikh2025]; an animal-tissue method paper confirms the false-positive problem and shows that fixing it raises the detection limit fifty-fold [tleg-almeida2026]; and a quality-screening framework concludes most human tissue studies fail basic contamination controls [tleg-lane2026]. The cardiovascular plaque cohort [tleg-marfella2024] rests on the same class of measurement. Until this is settled, no timing statement about microplastics is defensible.

**Does treating gums change metabolism?** Cochrane: HbA1c down 0.43 points at 3-4 months from 30 trials, moderate certainty [tleg-simpson2022], and 0.6 points at 12 months in a UK trial [tleg-daiuto2018]. Against that, the large multicentre US Diabetes and Periodontal Therapy Trial was stopped for futility, with no HbA1c difference at six months despite improved periodontal measures [tleg-engebretson2013].

**Dietary AGEs:** two weeks of a low-AGE diet raised clamp-measured insulin sensitivity [tleg-decourten2016], while a four-week trial in 73 people [tleg-linkens2022] and a six-week trial in healthy adults [tleg-semba2014] found nothing, despite confirmed differences in intake and excretion.

**Fried fat and endothelial function:** the same group that found used fryer fat impaired flow-mediated dilation [tleg-williams1999] later found no effect from oils used for eight hours of deep frying [tleg-williams2001]; the cohort data likewise split between a 22-37% excess of cardiovascular events [tleg-qin2021] and no association in a Spanish cohort frying in olive and sunflower oil [tleg-guallar2012].

**Seed oils:** blood linoleic acid is associated with lower cardiovascular disease across 30 cohorts [tleg-marklund2019], while recovered data from the Minnesota Coronary Experiment showed cholesterol lowering with no mortality benefit, and a pooled analysis of five trials was null [tleg-ramsden2016].

**NSAIDs and muscle:** maximal over-the-counter ibuprofen halved hypertrophy in young adults over eight weeks [tleg-lilja2018]; the same doses did not blunt, and appeared to help, gains in older adults over 12 weeks [tleg-trappe2011].

**Sunscreen and melanoma:** halved in the trial's 10-year follow-up [tleg-green2011]; no protective association in a meta-analysis of observational studies, which the authors attribute to confounding by sun sensitivity [tleg-rueegg2019].

**Methylmercury half-life:** 94 days crude versus 57 days after subtracting background, with the authors preferring the crude figure for practical use [tleg-yaginuma2012]. Cadmium half-life estimates span 13.6-23.5 years depending on sex and creatinine adjustment [tleg-suwazono2009].

**Red and processed meat:** IARC Group 1 and 2A [tleg-bouvard2015] against the NutriRECS panel's weak recommendation that adults continue current intake [tleg-johnston2019].

**Gas cooking:** a systematic review of 66 studies by consultancy-affiliated authors concludes the asthma literature is too weak for causal inference [tleg-li2023gas], while the strongest intervention data are an uncontrolled before-after stove-replacement study [tleg-sehgal2026].

## Practical bottom line

**Start now, because the clock is years long:**
- Stop adding PFAS (filtered drinking water, no fluorinated textiles or cookware coatings you are abrading). Expect 13-26% per year of decline once intake stops [tleg-li2018pfas]. If you have a high measured level, this is the one body burden with interventions that work on a months-not-years timescale, and NASEM's thresholds are 2 and 20 ng/mL summed PFAS [tleg-nasem2022, tleg-moller2024].
- Daily sunscreen. It is the slowest-paying and highest-value item here: no detectable photoageing over 4.5 years, 39% fewer squamous-cell tumours, benefits persisting 8-10 years after the trial ended [tleg-hughes2013, tleg-green1999, tleg-vanderpols2006, tleg-green2011].
- Stop smoking indoors and get out of other people's smoke; avoid cadmium's dominant source, which is tobacco [tleg-tellezplaza2012].

**Start three to six months before a conception attempt:**
- Cut high-mercury fish: blood mercury needs 4-6 months to fall substantially [tleg-yaginuma2012].
- Clean up the chemical exposures whose kinetics are fast but whose target takes a spermatogenic cycle: packaging and canned food [tleg-rudel2011], low-residue produce [tleg-chiu2016], processed meat [tleg-afeiche2014b].
- Treat periodontitis: systemic markers take 2-6 months, and there is a deliberate 24-48 hour worsening right after intensive treatment [tleg-tonetti2007].

**These can wait until two weeks before - and still work:**
- Reducing air-pollution exposure. The final days of sperm development (spermiation) have their own sensitive endpoints [tleg-russo2025], and for the egg the ovarian-stimulation window carries the strongest signal [tleg-gaskins2019].
- Switching food packaging and produce: urine responds in 1-3 days [tleg-rudel2011, tleg-lu2006].
- Stopping NSAIDs, if trying to conceive: ovulation recovers in the next cycle [tleg-smith1996], and use around conception carried a 1.89-fold miscarriage association [tleg-li2018nsaid].

**Must be daily or it does nothing:** HEPA filtration [tleg-chen2015], packaging and produce avoidance [tleg-carwile2011], topical vitamin C while building the skin reservoir [tleg-pinnell2001], water filtration [tleg-mackeown2024], prescribed statins and SGLT2 inhibitors [tleg-yourman2021, tleg-berg2021].

**Can drop to most-days once established:** topical retinoids, where three times weekly maintained a year of daily gains and once weekly did not [tleg-olsen1997b].

**Don't bother timing:** marinades and cooking-oil choice work on the meal in front of you and nothing else [tleg-smith2008, tleg-casal2010]; there is nothing to schedule, only to do each time you cook hot and dry.

**Don't do at all:** ozone therapy, where Cochrane's review of three small trials in 212 participants could draw no conclusions even for its best-studied use [tleg-liu2015ozone].

## What we don't know

- **Microplastic kinetics.** No human uptake rate, no clearance half-life, no demonstration that reducing exposure lowers any measured burden - and an unresolved argument about whether the tissue measurements are real [tleg-monikh2025, tleg-almeida2026, tleg-lane2026].
- **Whether any avoidance intervention changes an outcome.** Every biomarker-lowering study in this group stops at the biomarker [tleg-rudel2011, tleg-oates2014, tleg-moller2024, tleg-gasiorowski2022].
- **Sperm and egg time courses for most factors.** The air-pollution window studies [tleg-sun2020, tleg-russo2025] are the only properly time-resolved reproductive data here. For processed meat, antibiotics, PPIs, acetaminophen and microplastics, every "about three months" figure is extrapolated from the length of spermatogenesis, not measured.
- **The PPI semen lag.** Use 6-12 months before a semen analysis was associated with threefold higher odds of a low total motile count while recent use was not - a pattern the authors could not explain and nobody has replicated [tleg-huijgen2016].
- **Whether CoQ10 depletion matters.** Blood CoQ10 halves within a month of high-dose atorvastatin [tleg-rundek2004], and trials of replacing it for muscle symptoms disagree [tleg-qu2018].
- **Arsenic beyond urine.** Urinary arsenic reflects only the last few days [tleg-buchet1981]; there is no accepted long-term internal-dose marker, so "reducing arsenic exposure" has no measurable medium-term endpoint.
- **How long benefits from periodontal, air-quality and dietary improvements persist if the improvement lapses.** Almost no study in this group measured a washout period after a successful intervention.
